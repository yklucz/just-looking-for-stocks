How to read the evidence

Historical replay tests earlier dates using a saved model. It helps inspect behavior, but is not a record of forecasts issued in real time.

Prospective forecasts are recorded when issued. Their outcomes stay pending until the required market data arrives. Missing or corrected data is labeled separately.

A candidate is not a proven advantage. Missing metrics or too little baseline evidence do not establish an edge. Shadow models collect evidence; activation is a separate, manual decision.

Fixed universe bias: testing only today's chosen stocks leaves out companies that disappeared or were never selected. Results may not represent the wider market.

Period applies to Experiments and Forecast history. Model and evaluation kind apply to forecasts. Ticker applies to every tab.
